# Code of conduct

This project is for people who like making and using small, useful tools. To keep it pleasant:

- **Be kind and assume good faith.** Disagree about the code, not the person.
- **Be patient with newcomers.** Everyone here started somewhere, and "how do I…?" is a fine question.
- **Keep it on topic and legal.** No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or spam. Do not use the project to ask for, share or promote infringement of someone else's copyright or access to paid content.
- **Respect privacy.** Do not post other people's personal information, and do not paste signed or private links in issues.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who keep doing it.

To report a problem, use a [private security advisory](https://github.com/FernandoHN99/flux-downloader/security/advisories/new) and say it is about conduct, or contact a maintainer through their GitHub profile.

This is adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
